## Full narration, ready to record

“What if refusing to share a few rats brought an entire land to famine? Kange and Gbise were two brothers who lived near each other but never lived in peace. Their insults were common. Then the young men met Akoodo, feared across Saghev for witchcraft and sorcery. Hunters met him after catching rats. They greeted him, but refused his request for their catch. Akoodo said, ‘Since you refused to give me the rats, rats are all you will eat.’ He released them, and the land was overrun. Thousands filled every hole. Crops and stored food were destroyed. The elders asked Atongee, the eye of the gods, for answers. The curse came from Akoodo. The young men brought roasted rats and knelt. Akoodo lifted the curse, but families had lost loved ones and livelihoods. Read *The Infested Land* by Benjamin Armstrong Zugu. Click to read.”
