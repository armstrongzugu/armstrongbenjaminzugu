# Benjamin Armstrong Zugu — Writing Archive

The portfolio is a minimal, writing-only scroll narrative inspired by the reference site’s sparse full-viewport chapters and central focus. The current presentation uses a deep charcoal canvas so the writing can carry the contrast. Benjamin Armstrong Zugu’s display name is smaller and more restrained than the earlier version, while navigation, chapter labels, metadata, and work labels use a bright gold/coral accent system that pops against the dark field.

Typography is limited to **EB Garamond/Garamond** for literary display text and **Montserrat** for labels, metadata, navigation, and interface copy. Publications, poems, books, and research works are shown as dark translucent cards rather than white panels. Each work occupies its own near-full-screen scene. An IntersectionObserver toggles active scenes: the current work resolves from blur and low opacity into focus, then dissolves back out as the reader leaves it so the next work can resolve in. Orbit-like Tiv-inspired line geometry and the Shakespeare sketch move subtly with pointer depth, while reduced-motion users receive a static, fully visible fallback.

Teaching, tutoring, classroom, and departmental material remains removed. Resume details are limited to writing-relevant education, literary recognition, affiliations, place, and languages, and appear in the lower author record. Future stories and poems can be added as additional individual work scenes in `src/main.js`.

The project remains a static Vite site. `src/main.js` owns the works sequence and scroll observer; `src/styles.css` owns the Garamond/Montserrat system, dark high-contrast palette, centered stage, dissolve motion, responsive layout, and Tiv textile-inspired line system; `src/shakespeare-sketch.webp` provides the bundled background for GitHub Pages; `dist/` is the Pages artifact.
